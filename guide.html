3-Step Windshield Application Protocol

Takes 30 Seconds
1

Clean Glass Surface

Wipe the inside bottom-left corner of your windshield with a dry microfiber cloth to remove dust, oil film, or moisture.

2

Peel 3M Liner

Slowly peel the protective backing film from one corner, exposing the high-tack automotive acrylic adhesive.

3

Firmly Press from Center

Position the sticker upright on glass. Press firmly outwards from the center to eliminate any micro air pockets.
